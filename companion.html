Chapter 1

D Discovering Your Path

Your path becomes clearer through small decisions that match your values and interests.

Reflect

Which choice in front of you best matches the person you want to become?

Take Action

Run one 20-minute path test today: practice a skill, research a role, or ask someone what the work is really like.

Chapter 2

R Relationships and Support Systems

Strong support, honest feedback, healthy boundaries, and respect help you grow.

Reflect

Who in your circle encourages you, tells you the truth, and respects your goals?

Take Action

Text that person today: thank them, share one goal, and ask for a 15-minute check-in this week.

Chapter 3

E Education and Lifelong Learning

School, work, hobbies, and practice can all build skills that keep compounding.

Reflect

For the path you want next, what skill or credential is required, and what is the lowest-cost way to earn it?

Take Action

Compare one college, trade, certificate, or work-based program today. Write down its total cost, time to finish, aid options, and the skill you would gain.

Chapter 4

A Adaptability and Resilience

Waiting is not wasted when you keep learning, stay ready, and choose your response.

Reflect

What setback or waiting season are you in, and what skill can you build while you wait?

Take Action

Make a two-column note titled "What I can control" and "My next move," then complete the first move today.

Chapter 5

M Monetary Success

Good credit, steady saving, and informed choices protect your future options.

Reflect

If an unexpected $500 bill arrived tomorrow, how much could you cover without borrowing?

Take Action

Use First Money Moves to set a starter emergency-fund target, then schedule one transfer of at least $5 for your next payday.

Use with this chapter: First Money Moves Calculators Money Visualizer Glossary
Chapter 6

B Building a Career

A career grows when you learn the whole operation, solve problems, and build transferable skills.

Reflect

Which transferable skill can you already prove with a real example from school, work, sports, or service?

Take Action

Save three job postings for one role you may want. Note the skill all three share and one way you can build or prove it this month.

Chapter 7

I Inner Fulfillment Through Sharing

Sharing what you know deepens your learning and can make someone else's path easier.

Reflect

What do you know now that would have helped you one year ago?

Take Action

Send one person a three-step note that teaches that skill today.

Chapter 8

G Giving Back

Use what you have to strengthen your community and open a door for someone behind you.

Reflect

What need at school, work, church, or in your neighborhood matches a skill you have?

Take Action

Contact one school, church, or community group today and offer one hour of a skill you can share.

Keep Going

Make the next honest decision

Build the next skill. Strengthen the next relationship. Serve the next person. Then return when it is time for another step.
