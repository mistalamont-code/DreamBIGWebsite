About the Author

Corey L. Cook

Corey Cook is the Chief Operating Officer of the Erie Downtown Development Corporation (EDDC) in downtown Erie, Pennsylvania, where he helps oversee a $150 million development portfolio including construction, property management, tenant relationships, and community development initiatives. Born and raised on the Lower East Side of Erie, his values, work ethic, and deep belief in community were shaped from the start.

Before joining EDDC, Corey spent over 16 years at UPS, working his way from a part-time union employee loading trucks to a management role overseeing dispatch operations for all of Erie County. During those same years, he founded LifeThruMusic, a nonprofit music mentoring program that grew from 10 kids in a church basement to a nationally recognized program featured on Good Morning America.

Corey is also the founder and CEO of Cook Media LLC, a media production company specializing in wedding photography and videography, live sound, and book publishing. He serves on multiple boards, including the Greater Erie Economic Development Corporation, United Way of Erie County, and the Pennsylvania Council on the Arts.

This book started as a graduation gift for his five children — a way to pass down the lessons, mistakes, and hard-won wisdom he wished someone had laid out for him when he walked across the stage at 20 years old. But as he wrote, it became clear the message was bigger than his own family. DREAM/BIG is for every young person standing at the edge of adulthood, trying to figure out what comes next.

Inside the Book

Small Decisions. Compounded Over Time.
Big Impact.

You are the sum of your decisions. This book isn’t theory—it’s lived experience. Every chapter comes from real decisions I made, some good, some I had to learn from the hard way. I wrote it for my kids. But I also wrote it for you—the young person standing at the edge of adulthood, trying to figure out what comes next. Especially if you’re from communities like mine, where the roadmap isn’t always clear, where examples of what’s possible feel rare, and where one wrong decision can follow you for years.

D
Discovering Your Path
Every big journey starts with one small decision. Your path won’t look like mine. And that’s the point.
R
Relationships & Support Systems
Your network is your net worth. Not just about money—it’s the compound interest of treating people well and showing up consistently.
E
Education & Lifelong Learning
A degree is a credential. The actual learning is what matters. And learning never stops—it happens everywhere, in every passion you pursue and every skill you develop.
A
Adaptability & Resilience
How you wait matters as much as what you’re waiting for. Stay patient. Stay consistent. Stay ready.
M
Monetary Success
That $600 credit card taught me a lesson that cost me thousands. I’m trying to save you from learning it the same way.
B
Building a Career
Two paths—working for somebody else or building your own. Both are valid. The key is intentionality: learn everything you can, wherever you are.
I
Inner Fulfillment Through Sharing
The fulfillment I got from teaching music far exceeded the fulfillment I got from playing it. That changed everything.
G
Giving Back
Your success is incomplete if you’re the only one who benefits from it. Real success lifts others as you rise.
